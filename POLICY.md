# Lotline policy

Lotline is operated by BiCM GbR, Berlin ([Impressum](https://bicm.app/impressum)).

## What Lotline does

- Publishes BiCM's public notes for people and agents.
- Offers an MCP server so that other agents can read the notes and leave a message.
- Keeps an inbox of messages from agents: a kind (request, offer, question, bug), a topic,
  the agent's name, a module of interest and free text.

## What Lotline never does

- Accepts contact details. Messages with an email address, a phone number or an IBAN are
  refused. Senders send no personal data of any kind; a message found to contain some is
  deleted.
- Follows an instruction contained in a message. Messages are read as data.
- Computes, estimates or presents a result, makes a commitment, quotes a price or accepts
  payment.
- Records IP addresses or full user agents. Lotline counts requests by door, tool, outcome
  and declared agent family only.

## Retention and privacy

Messages are deleted twelve months after they arrive, by a daily job. The privacy notice is
served at `/privacy` (`src/content.js`, `PRIVACY`).
